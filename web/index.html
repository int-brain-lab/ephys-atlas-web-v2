About the project

IBL Ephys Atlas

The IBL Ephys Atlas displays regional electrophysiology summaries, voxel-scale features, and brain-wide analysis results on the mouse brain atlas.

Developed by the International Brain Laboratory.

For dataset sources, release information, and provenance, open Data details in the viewer. Use these records when citing the data.
