About the project

IBL Ephys Atlas

The IBL Ephys Atlas brings together task-independent electrophysiology features, Brain-Wide Map task-event responses, and anatomy datasets such as AGEA in one spatial viewer.

Developed by the International Brain Laboratory, with anatomy data from external sources including the Allen Institute.

For dataset sources, release information, and provenance, open Data details in the viewer. Use these records when citing the data.
