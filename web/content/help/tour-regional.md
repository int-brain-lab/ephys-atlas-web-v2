## Choose a dataset and feature {#choose-data}

Choose a dataset in Data, check its edition or exact version in Release, then search Feature. Display & parcellation above the brain views controls regional or volume display and the anatomical mapping.

## Navigate linked slices {#navigate}

Drag a slice control, scroll over a view, or use the arrow keys. Coronal, sagittal, and horizontal views stay linked to one atlas location.

## Inspect and select regions {#inspect}

Hover over a brain view to inspect a region and its value. Click a region in the brain or Regions panel to add it to your selection.

## Understand the values {#interpret}

Use the global distribution to see where values lie. Selected regions can also be compared without changing the underlying data.

## Verify, share, or download {#verify-share}

Open Info to check units, population, release, and provenance. Share copies this exact view; Download exports the feature data.
