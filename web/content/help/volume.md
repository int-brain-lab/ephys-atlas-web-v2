## Inspect voxels {#volume-inspection}

Hover over a registered brain view to inspect the nearest voxel and its value. Outside and missing voxels are not treated as scientific zero values.

## Use the anatomy overlay {#volume-anatomy}

Anatomical outlines and the selected parcellation help identify the surrounding region. They do not change the volume grid or voxel values.

## Read the volume distribution {#volume-distribution}

Global Distribution describes valid voxels in the selected feature volume. Settings control the scale, color range, opacity, and anatomy outlines.


**Auto-slice** in Regions moves the three linked slices to show a selected region. Uncheck it to keep slices fixed while changing regions. Your choice is included in shared URLs. Manual slice navigation always remains available.
