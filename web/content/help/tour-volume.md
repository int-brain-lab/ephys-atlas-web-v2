## Choose a dataset and feature {#choose-data}

Choose a dataset in Data, then search Feature. Data details contains the exact version and provenance. Display & parcellation above the brain views controls regional or volume display and the anatomical mapping.

## Navigate linked slices {#navigate}

Drag a slice control, scroll over a view, or use the arrow keys. Coronal, sagittal, and horizontal views stay linked to one atlas location.

## Inspect a voxel {#inspect}

Hover over a brain view to inspect the voxel at the current atlas location. Missing or outside voxels are not treated as scientific zero values.

## Understand the values {#interpret}

Use the global distribution to understand the value range. Visualization settings change the display, not the volume grid or source voxel values.

## Verify, share, or download {#verify-share}

Open Data details to check units, population, release, and provenance. Share copies this exact view; Download exports the feature data.
