The IBL Ephys Atlas provides a shared anatomical framework for exploring regional summaries, voxel-scale electrophysiology features, and brain-wide analysis results.

You can also author a schema-v1 dataset and import its ZIP archive into browser-local storage. Importing a local dataset does not upload it or make it available to other people.

The Ephys Atlas is developed by [IBL Core](https://iblcore.org/).
