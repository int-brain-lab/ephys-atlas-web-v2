Explore how an electrophysiology feature varies across the mouse brain.

## Quick Start {#quick-start}

1. **Choose what to view.** Select a dataset, then a feature. Regional shows summaries by brain region; Volume shows values across voxels.
2. **Move through the brain.** Drag a slice control, scroll over a view, or use the arrow keys. Coronal, sagittal, and horizontal stay linked to one atlas location.
3. **Inspect a value.** Hover over the brain to inspect a region or voxel. For regional data, click a region in the brain or Regions panel to select it.
4. **Interpret and save.** Use the distribution and comparison to understand the values. Open Info for units, population, release, and provenance. Share preserves the current view; Download exports its data.

> Display controls change how values look. They do not change the source or downloaded values.
