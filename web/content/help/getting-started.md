Explore how an electrophysiology feature varies across the mouse brain.

## Quick Start {#quick-start}

1. **Choose what to view.** Select a dataset, then a feature. Regional shows summaries by brain region; Volume shows values across voxels.
2. **Move through the brain.** Drag a slice control, scroll over a view, or use the arrow keys. Coronal, sagittal, and horizontal stay linked to one atlas location.
3. **Inspect a value.** Hover over the brain to inspect a region or voxel. For regional data, click a region in the brain or a leaf in Regions to select it. Click parent rows in Regions to expand or collapse their children.
4. **Interpret and save.** Use the distribution and comparison to understand the values. Open Data details for units, population, release, and provenance. Share preserves the current view; Download exports its data.

> Display controls change how values look. They do not change the source or downloaded values.

**Auto-slice** in Regions moves the three linked slices to an interior position near the selected region’s center. Click the target icon beside the fold/unfold buttons to switch it off and keep slices fixed. Hover or focus the icon for its current state and help. Your choice is included in shared URLs. Manual slice navigation always remains available.
