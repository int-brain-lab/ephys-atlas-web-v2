# W42 channels, W46 volumes and edges deployment

Status: complete on 2026-11-15.

The D100 selection is live at <https://ephys-atlas.iblcore.org>. It publishes
the `ea_active` `2026_W42` channels and a new `2026_W46` encoding volume and its
edges as immutable releases, and promotes one catalog change that makes them
the Ephys Atlas default edition. Every earlier release and edition remains in
the catalog. Brain-Wide Map stays out by owner decision (D100); the D094
restoration remains unpromoted.

All three were built on macOS arm64 at commit
`88246e054e29e6eedb1d844fa87f240f4a23715f` (PR #19, fast-forwarded to `main`
so the builder commit is on `main`), with ibleatools `647d7ba` and iblatlas
`b88da5d`. They passed `just production-release-preflight` and were published
with `tools.s3_publish --apply --transport sdk`. The catalog was compiled and
promoted with `tools.s3_catalog --apply`, after its production viewer check,
against local copies of every referenced release.

Before this deployment the unpromoted 2026-11-04 releases were deleted from
the production root (D100).

## Consequences

- The default view is `rms_ap` on the W42 channels.
- Volume features carry the ibleatools denoised-schema descriptions.
- The W46 volume covers the same 4,048,869 voxels as W39.

## Production checks

`node web/scripts/verify-production-site.mjs https://ephys-atlas.iblcore.org`
passed against the live site (landing, geometry, navigation, quantiles, URL,
static projections, catalog inventory, data chooser, all seven datasets and the
3-D mesh), loading the W42 channels and the W46 volumes and edges by default.
`just site-compatibility` passes. This is automated Chromium evidence only. The
first site push (`bdbb7e9`) failed CI because the development-bundle parity
test still pinned v7; v8 fixed it before any site deployment.
